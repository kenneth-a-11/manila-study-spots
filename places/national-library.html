National Library of the Philippines

About This Location

Located in Ermita, Manila near TUP, the National Library offers spacious, quiet reading rooms with free admission. It is the ideal environment for deep focus, long reading sessions, and academic research.

Feature Specification

Hours 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Mon–Fri)

Perks Air-conditioned, wide desks, research materials
